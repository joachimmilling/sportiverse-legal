Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sportiverse. Can't find your answer? Contact support@sportiverse.no.

What is Sportiverse?

Sportiverse is an all-in-one sports app for clubs, leagues, and players. Create a club, schedule matches, score them live, and follow standings that update automatically — all in one place.

Is Sportiverse free?

Yes — Sportiverse is free, and always will be.

Which sports does Sportiverse support?

Football — wherever you play it. The ambition is every sport, everywhere.

Who is Sportiverse for?

Coaches, players, and fans — three audiences, equally served. Coaches organize squads and matchdays, players follow their stats and career, and fans track live scores and standings.

How is Sportiverse different from apps like Spond or TeamSnap?

Most team apps focus on organizing — scheduling, messaging, and payments. Sportiverse starts with the match itself: live scoring, automatic statistics and standings, player ratings, and a feed built from real match data. You run the club and the league in the same app the match lives in.

Can youth teams use Sportiverse?

Yes — grassroots football includes youth football. Players need to be at least 13 to have their own account, and users under 18 need parental consent.

Does Sportiverse track player and team statistics?

Yes. Record match events — goals, assists, cards, free kicks, corners, penalties — essentially everything the referee whistles. Every statistic follows automatically from there: minutes played, career totals, club records. No spreadsheets, no manual entry.

How do player ratings work?

Ratings are calculated from recorded match events, not opinions or votes. Every rating is backed by what actually happened on the pitch.

Can fans follow matches live?

Yes. Match events are scored live in the app, so supporters see goals, cards, and substitutions in real time — whether they're on the sideline or across the country. Live coverage is score and event updates — like a live ticker for your club's matches.

How does Sportiverse keep fans engaged?

Match highlights, results, and milestones are shared automatically to a social feed where fans can like and comment. Goals and wins come with celebrations — it's a sports app, not a spreadsheet.

Can I share to other social platforms?

Yes. Results, highlights, and milestones from the feed can be shared straight from the app to other social platforms.

Can I track training attendance?

Yes. Schedule training sessions, track who attends, and keep the whole squad prepared and in sync.

Can I run a league on Sportiverse?

Yes. Create a league, invite clubs, and standings update automatically from match results — with configurable point systems and multiple divisions.

Is Sportiverse available on iOS and Android?

Yes. Sportiverse runs on both iOS and Android, and everything syncs in real time across devices — the whole club sees the same thing regardless of platform. Sportiverse is mobile-first — there is no web version yet.

What languages does Sportiverse support?

The app is available in English, Norwegian, Danish, and Swedish.

How do I get the app?

On iOS, join the beta through TestFlight. On Android, request access and we'll add you to the test group.

How does Sportiverse handle privacy and my data?

Sportiverse follows GDPR. Data for EU users is stored in Europe, and you can request access to or deletion of your data at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details, or contact privacy@sportiverse.no.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete individual content and your entire account directly in the app, under Settings → Account Center. The delete account page covers all options, including deletion by email.
